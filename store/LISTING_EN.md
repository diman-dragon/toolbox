**Full description:**
Multitool by DataLaw Design is a set of measuring tools in one app. It works offline, with no ads and no sign-up.

• Level – bubble and vial, calm readings, two-position calibration
• Protractor – angle between surfaces and slope; 15° · 30° · 45° · 60° · 90° templates
• Compass and metal detector (magnetometer)
• Height meter and rangefinder – via the camera, with an error estimate
• Plumb line – a camera-based vertical
• Ruler with bank-card calibration
• Sound meter – dB(A), Fast/Slow, min/avg/max
• QR and barcode scanner

Highlights:
• Three themes: dark, AMOLED and light “Paper”; Russian and English

The camera is used by the height meter, rangefinder, plumb line and scanner; the microphone by the sound meter. Frames and audio are processed on-device only and never stored. Accuracy depends on your phone's sensors; use professional instruments for critical measurements.
